About the clinic

Quality meets humanity.

We chose this line as our promise. It means precise clinical work, and patients who feel listened to at every step.

Our approach

A smile is part of a face. We plan for both.

Straight teeth that don’t suit your profile are only half a result. That is the idea behind our name: every plan considers your lips, profile and smile line along with your bite.

Diagnosis before treatment

Photos, X-rays and a full bite exam, so the plan is based on what we measured, not on a guess.

Planned for your face

Teeth, lips and profile are treated as one picture. A straight smile should also look natural from the side.

Time to listen

Nervous patients are welcome. Your first visit is a conversation, and nothing starts until you agree.

The doctor

Dr. Kareem Moustafa Kamel

Founder · Orthodontics & dental implants

Dr. Kareem founded Braces & Faces in Smouha to offer orthodontic care that is precise in the chair and patient outside it.

Every case is examined, planned and followed up by him personally, from a child’s first check at age seven to adults replacing a missing tooth with an implant. He explains each option in plain language, so you can decide with confidence.
