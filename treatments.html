Treatments

Modern solutions for every age.

Every plan starts with a full assessment, so you only receive the treatment you actually need. Here is what we offer and who each option suits.

Braces Clear aligners Early orthodontics Dental implants Retainers

Teens & adults · simple to complex cases

Metal & ceramic braces

Fixed braces give precise control over every tooth, which makes them the most reliable option for complex cases.

  • Metal braces: strong, efficient and the most economical
  • Ceramic braces: tooth-coloured brackets that are far less visible
  • Adjustment visits roughly every 4–6 weeks
  • Suitable at any age, as long as gums and bone are healthy

Suitable mild–moderate cases

Clear aligners

A series of removable, nearly invisible trays that move your teeth step by step.

  • Eat what you like and brush normally
  • Worn about 22 hours a day for the best result
  • Hardly noticeable at work, at university or in photos
  • We will tell you honestly if braces would work better for your case

Children from age 7

Early orthodontics

Seven is the right age for a first orthodontic check, while the jaws are still growing.

  • Spots crossbites, crowding and jaw problems early
  • Growth can be guided, which often makes later treatment shorter
  • Most children only need monitoring at this stage
  • A friendly first visit that builds confidence, not fear

Replacing missing teeth

Dental implants

A stable, natural-looking replacement for a missing tooth, built on healthy roots.

  • Looks, feels and works like a natural tooth
  • Protects neighbouring teeth, unlike a traditional bridge
  • Planned with X-rays to check the bone first
  • Can be combined with orthodontics to open the right space

After every treatment

Retainers & aftercare

The final stage that protects your result. Teeth naturally try to move back, and retainers stop them.

  • Custom fixed or removable retainers
  • Scheduled check-ups after braces come off
  • Clear instructions on how long to wear them

Myth or fact

Three things patients often get wrong.

Myth
“I’m too old for braces.”

Teeth move at any age as long as the gums and bone are healthy. Many people start orthodontic treatment as adults.

Myth
“Seven is too early to see an orthodontist.”

Seven is the right age for a first check. Most children won’t need braces yet, but early guidance can prevent bigger problems later.

Fact
“Missed appointments make treatment longer.”

Each adjustment builds on the last. Skipped visits, broken brackets and unworn elastics can add months.
